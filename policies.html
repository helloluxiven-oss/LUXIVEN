03 — Delivery

Shipping Policy

We ship worldwide. Because each piece is curated and quality-checked before dispatch, please allow a short processing window before your order ships.

Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days of successful payment. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking once your order is dispatched.

Delivery Timelines

  • India: 5–10 business days after dispatch
  • United States, United Kingdom, UAE, Australia: 8–15 business days after dispatch
  • Rest of world: 10–20 business days after dispatch

Timelines are estimates and may vary with customs clearance, destination and courier. Larger items (furniture) may require additional time.

Courier & Tracking

We ship via established international couriers and logistics partners (such as DHL, FedEx, and regional carriers). A tracking number is provided for every order once dispatched.

Shipping Costs

  • Orders over $200 (or equivalent): complimentary standard shipping
  • Orders under $200: shipping calculated and shown at checkout before payment
  • Customs duties / import taxes, where applicable, are the responsibility of the recipient unless stated otherwise at checkout
04 — Returns & Refunds

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. The following terms apply to cancellations, returns and refunds.

Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled for a full refund within 24 hours of placement, provided the order has not yet been dispatched. To cancel, email us with your order number. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled but may be returned under the terms below.

Returns & Exchange Eligibility

  • Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery
  • Items must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging
  • Made-to-order, customised or final-sale items are not eligible unless they arrive damaged or defective
  • To start a return, email your order number and reason; we will share return instructions

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

Refund Timeframe

Once a returned item is received and inspected (typically 2–4 business days), approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. Refunds reflect within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer. Original shipping charges are non-refundable except where the item was damaged, defective, or incorrect.
